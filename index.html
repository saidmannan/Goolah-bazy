POWER

Traditional Afghan Team Game

Goolah
Bazy

Four mats in a line. Four heavy goolahs. Two teams of two.

  • 4 mats in a straight line
  • 2 teams of two players
  • 4 metallic goolahs
  • 21 points to win

One team throws, the other defends. Both throwers use the same line behind mat 1; the defenders start behind mats 1 and 3. A Tir or a Mat earns another throw. Roles swap every round, and the first team to 21 wins.

How to play

  1. Drag up from your goolah and release to throw it down the line of mats.
  2. Drag left or right while aiming to steer the throw across the line.
  3. The longer the drag, the harder the throw. A hard throw can carry past mat 4.
  4. Turn order: throwing P1, throwing P2, defending P1, defending P2. Both throwers use the same line behind mat 1; defenders start behind mats 1 and 3.
  5. Tir (hitting an opponent goolah) scores 1 point and one extra throw.
  6. Mat: a goolah resting in an open hollow captures that mat. Each team's first two mats in a round score nothing; every further mat scores 1 point.
  7. Mat-per: you may hold a goolah in the mat it captured and keep the chance for your next turn. An opponent who hits it scores 3 if it stays in the hollow, 7 if it is knocked out.
  8. Hitting your own teammate scores nothing: their goolah goes back where it was.
  9. The round ends when all four mats are closed; the player who closes the last mat gets one final throw at one opponent. Roles then swap.

First team to 21 points wins. A goolah that stops somewhere unplayable is moved to the nearest playable spot on the way back to the field.
