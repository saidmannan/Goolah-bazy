Traditional Afghan Team Game

Goolah
Bazy

Four mats in a line. Four heavy goolahs. Two teams of two.

  • 4 mats in a straight line
  • 2 mats for each team
  • 4 metallic goolahs
  • 21 points to win

Mats 1 and 3 belong to Shānanda, mats 2 and 4 to Orange. Both players of the throwing team throw from the same line behind mat 1. Traditional scoring rules will be wired in from your rule set — until then the scoreboard is kept by hand.

How to play

  1. Drag up from your goolah and release to throw it down the line of mats.
  2. Drag left or right while aiming to steer the throw across the line.
  3. The longer the drag, the harder the throw. The dotted path and the ring show where it is expected to stop, and a hard throw can carry past mat 4.
  4. Both players of one team throw before the other team plays: Shānanda P1, Shānanda P2, then Orange P1, Orange P2.
  5. Both players throw from the same line behind mat 1.
  6. Goolahs stay on the ground, so a later throw can roll into an earlier one and knock it away or into a mat.
  7. Mats are shallow dishes in the dirt, 5 cm deep. Mats 1 and 3 are Shānanda's, mats 2 and 4 are Orange's.

Scoring: the traditional rule set has not been added yet, so no rule has been invented — points are recorded by hand with the + and − buttons on each team card. First team to 21 wins.
